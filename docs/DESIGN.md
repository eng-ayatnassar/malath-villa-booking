## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene luxury and timeless architecture of private sanctuary rentals. Drawing inspiration from Mediterranean coastal estates and contemporary Levantine retreats, the aesthetic balances sun-warmed organic materials with modern editorial discipline.

The visual style is **Understated Coastal Luxury**:
- **Generous Spatial Rhythm:** Unhurried negative space evoking architectural volumes and private courtyards.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise, functional sans-serif metadata and currency indicators.
- **Natural Tactility:** Terracotta and sunbaked stone accents grounded by deep marine slate tones, avoiding artificial neon saturation.
- **Restrained Elevation:** Minimalist hairline borders, subtle warm-toned ambient diffusion, and floating translucent glass layers that defer visual priority to photography and architectural detail.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Symmetrical typographical metrics accommodating seamless bidirectional flow (LTR and RTL) for Arabic and Latin scripts without visual imbalance.

## Colors

The palette derives its richness from raw architectural materials: terracotta clay, coastal slate, limestone wash, and polished brass.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#D28E58`):** Terracotta Gold. Used strictly for prime calls-to-action, availability confirmations, curated tags, and active navigation indicators. Hover state transitions to `#BA7945`.
- **Secondary / Deep Slate (`#0F1E2E`):** Marine Slate. Anchor for high-priority typography, prominent icons, structural dividers, and primary contrast elements.
- **Tertiary / Muted Slate (`#5E6D7E`):** Secondary metadata, amenities, dates, subtext, and inactive UI boundaries.
- **Canvas / Ivory Cream (`#FBFBF9`):** Soft foundational background minimizing optical fatigue compared to stark white.
- **Surface / Card (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground panels rimmed with low-contrast limestone borders (`#EAE7E1`).

### Dark Mode Adaptation
- **Dark Canvas:** Midnight Slate (`#0B131C`).
- **Dark Surface Card:** Elevated Dark Marine (`#13202E`) bounded by structural hairline frames (`#22354A`).
- **Dark Typography:** Soft Alabaster (`#E8ECF1`) primary, muted slate gray secondary.
- **Dark Accent:** Warm Glowing Terracotta (`#E09D67`).

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial authority of high-contrast serif display letterforms with clean geometric numerals and interactive controls.

- **Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for villa collection titles, bespoke location intros, and hero quotes. Avoid using the serif face for small metadata, calendar dates, or technical property attributes.
- **Interface Workhorse (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Carries all body copy, search inputs, booking date pickers, amenities checklists, and rates. The generous x-height maintains supreme legibility across both Latin and companion Arabic font systems.
- **Numbers & Rates (`price-display`):** Numerical rates, seasonal availability counters, and guest quotas use tabular figures with tightened tracking to project precision and clarity.
- **Casing & Kerning:** Uppercase treatment is restricted to `label-md` and `label-lg` with open letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for categorical chips, luxury tiers, and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors high-end architectural planning: open vistas, deliberate structural framing, and clear circulation paths.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max content constraint of `1440px`, with outer canvas margins of `5rem` (`margin-desktop`) and gutters of `2.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Section separations favor generous spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to let property photography breathe.
- Component-level spacing uses a modular 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`) maintaining exact spatial relationships between property tags, rate disclosures, and host credentials.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface stacking and translucent materials rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a bright, sun-drenched atmosphere.

- **Hairline Framing:** Primary depth cues use 1px (or 0.5px high-DPI) borders colored in `#EAE7E1` (light mode) and `#22354A` (dark mode) instead of harsh dark outlines.
- **Sunlit Diffusion (Low-Elevation):** Subtle atmospheric shadow for idle property cards:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 30, 46, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 30, 46, 0.02)`.
- **Raised Sanctuary (High-Elevation / Modals & Float Bars):**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(15, 30, 46, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 30, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Mediterranean Frosted Glass:** Floating filter bars, sticky booking headers, and luxury concierge panels employ backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with `rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.85)` in light mode and `rgba(11, 19, 28, 0.85)` in dark mode, coupled with a 1px border.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** architectural profile (`roundedness: 1`), honoring the clean geometric edges of Mediterranean stone villas while softening high-frequency interactive touchpoints.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-inputs, checkboxes, image tags, and calendar date cells.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** The standard for villa imagery, interactive buttons, search inputs, dropdown panels, and property cards.
- **Extended Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Reserved for floating navigation bars, booking modals, and overarching container surfaces.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Exclusively utilized for status chips ("Superhost", "Instant Booking"), curated amenity badges, and circular media controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Terracotta Gold (`#D28E58`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border radius, subtle hover scale (`scale(1.01)`) and transition to `#BA7945`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary / Architectural:** Deep Marine Slate (`#0F1E2E`) background with alabaster text, or Ivory surface with 1px border (`#EAE7E1`), subtle hover fill shift to `#F5F3EF`.
- **Ghost / Text:** Underlined serif accents or tracking-spaced uppercase sans-serif with an animated terracotta underline.

### Property Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white card surface (`#FFFFFF`), 1px hairline border (`#EAE7E1`), `0.5rem` outer radius.
- **Visuals:** 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio gallery with continuous image fade on carousel hover. Floating heart wishlist icon encased in frosted glass circle.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Location title in `title-md`, bedroom/capacity metrics in muted slate `body-sm`, and price anchored in `price-display` alongside `/ night` label.

### Floating Search & Filter Bar
- Translucent backdrop blur (`16px`), rounded pill or `0.75rem` frame, rimmed by 1px `#EAE7E1`.
- Divided into distinct clickable segments (Destination, Dates, Guests) separated by vertical 1px hairline dividers.
- Active search trigger button rendered in compact Terracotta Gold with a minimalist search icon.

### Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Fields:** Clean limestone backgrounds (`#F5F3EE`), 1px border (`#EAE7E1`), transitioning to `#D28E58` outline on focus without heavy ring blooms.
- **Calendar:** Dual-month architectural view with terracotta-tinted selection ranges and high-contrast date typography.

### Badges & Chips
- Curated luxury tags (e.g., "Sea View", "Private Pool", "Bespoke Concierge") set in `label-md` uppercase with `0.5rem` or full pill radius, soft tinted background (`rgba(210, 142, 88, 0.08)`), and Terracotta Gold text (`#BA7945`).

### Bilingual & Directional Adaptations
- Layouts flip gracefully across the vertical axis for RTL (Arabic). Typography pairs seamlessly with Arabic serif display variants while retaining standard Latin numeral rates for consistent financial readability.
